## 1. Exit criteria, original and revised

The criteria were revised after seeing the step 4 results, because step 4 scored every rule finding against planted faults only, so a rule finding at a site that really breaches its threshold (a natural breach, such as a synthetic median entry lag of 7.4 days against the 7-day threshold) counted as a false positive: an error in the measurement, not in the agent.
